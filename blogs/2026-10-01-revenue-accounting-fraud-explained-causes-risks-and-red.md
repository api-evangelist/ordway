Revenue accounting fraud is the intentional manipulation of financial statements to misrepresent when or how much revenue a company has earned. Unlike honest accounting errors, fraud involves deliberate deception—booking sales that never happened, recognizing revenue before it's earned, or hiding terms that would delay recognition.
