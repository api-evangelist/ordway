A SaaS renewal pipeline is a structured CRM workflow that tracks existing customers from contract signing through renewal, applying the same stage-gated rigor you'd use for new business. Instead of calendar reminders that surface a few weeks before expiration, a pipeline provides visibility into what's coming, who owns it, and where deals stand.
