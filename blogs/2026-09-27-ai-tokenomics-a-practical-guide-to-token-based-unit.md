AI tokenomics is the practice of measuring, managing, and optimizing the consumption of AI tokens—the fundamental units of text that large language models read and generate—to connect variable intelligence costs directly to business value. Unlike traditional software with fixed per-seat pricing, AI costs scale with every prompt, every response, and every reasoning step your application performs.
