A billing address is the official address tied to your credit or debit card, used by banks and payment processors to verify your identity and prevent fraud. A shipping address is simply the physical destination where you want your purchased items delivered. While they can be the same location, they serve entirely different purposes in a transaction—and confusing them leads to declined payments or misdelivered packages.
