EV/NTM is a forward-looking valuation metric that compares a company's Enterprise Value to its projected financial results—typically revenue or EBITDA—for the Next Twelve Months. For SaaS companies, where growth often outpaces profitability, this multiple has become the standard benchmark investors use to assess relative value.
