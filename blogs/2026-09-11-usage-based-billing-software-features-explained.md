Usage-based billing software automates the process of charging customers based on actual consumption—API calls, compute hours, transactions, or data volume—rather than fixed subscription fees. The platform handles metering, applies pricing rules through a rating engine, generates detailed invoices, and posts revenue entries to your general ledger.
